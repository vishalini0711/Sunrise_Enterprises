About Sunrise Enterprises

Sunrise Enterprises was founded in 2018 by P. K. Singh, an electrical engineering graduate of BIT Sindri, after more than thirty years spent commissioning high-voltage switchyards and running power distribution for thermal plants, rolling mills, sponge iron units and pellet plants.

That field background shaped a deliberately small, hands-on setup: an SSI unit built beside B.I.T Sindri with its own winding, drying and testing lines, staffed by career transformer engineers and a younger team of mechanical engineers who keep the manufacturing floor running.

Rather than scale into a generic supplier, the firm has stayed focused on one thing — distribution transformers, built or rebuilt correctly the first time, with the paperwork and testing to prove it.
